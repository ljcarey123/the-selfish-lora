# Phase 3: Advanced Research Avenues - "The Digital Ecosystem"

## 🎯 Objective
With a robust simulation framework in place, this phase moves into more complex, open-ended research questions. The goal is to push the simulation towards a true digital ecosystem to study the emergence of complex social dynamics and behaviors.

---

## 🔬 Potential Research Avenues

### 1. Competition & Conflict
* **Mechanic:** Add a `kill` action, which costs energy but allows the victor to claim the loser's collected resources.
* **Research Focus:** Study the emergence of predator-prey dynamics, evolutionary arms races (e.g., better hunters vs. better evaders), and the balance between competitive and passive strategies.

### 2. Advanced Architectures (X-LoRA)
* **Mechanic:** Instead of a single LoRA, agents could use a framework like X-LoRA to dynamically mix the "skills" from a pool of expert adapters during the simulation.
* **Research Focus:** Investigate if this leads to the evolution of highly specialized agents (e.g., dedicated "scouts" and "gatherers") and complex task allocation without explicit programming.

### 3. Tribes & Kin Selection
* **Mechanic:** Introduce a "kin" or "tribe" identifier that is passed down through heredity.
* **Research Focus:** Investigate if agents evolve altruistic behaviors, such as sharing resources or signaling danger preferentially to their own kin, demonstrating the principles of kin selection.

---

## ✅ Success Criteria
* This phase is more exploratory. Success is defined by the emergence of complex, un-designed systemic behaviors.
* **Examples:**
  * Stable predator-prey population cycles.
  * Observable altruistic actions that decrease an individual's fitness but increase the fitness of its kin group.
  * Clear specialization of roles within the agent population.
